Different models use different amounts of energy

ℹ️ Why do models use different power?

AI models vary dramatically in energy use based on their size and type:

Model Type Power Why
Small (Haiku 4.5, Gemini Flash) 50-150W Fewer parameters = less computation
Medium (GPT-5.2 Instant, Sonnet 4.5) 150-350W Balanced size and capability
Large (GPT-5.2 Pro, Opus 4.5) 350-600W Most parameters = most compute
Image generation (DALL-E, SD) 300-600W GPU-intensive diffusion process
Video generation 800W+ Generates many frames sequentially
How we know this:
  • Luccioni et al. 2023 - Directly measured AI model energy consumption
  • Patterson et al. 2021 - Google's AI carbon emissions research
  • MLPerf - Industry-standard AI benchmarks

Note: These are estimates. Actual power varies based on hardware, datacenter efficiency, and batch size. We use conservative estimates from peer-reviewed research.

How we calculate emissions
CO₂ (g) = Energy (kWh) × PUE × Grid Intensity (g/kWh)
Energy (kWh) = Power (W) ÷ 1000 × Time (hours)
PUE = 1.2 (Power Usage Effectiveness - datacenter overhead)
Grid Intensity = Regional carbon intensity from electricity generation

  • Luccioni et al. 2023 - Measured AI model energy consumption directly
  • Patterson et al. 2021 - Google's foundational AI carbon emissions research
  • IEA - Grid carbon intensity data by region
  • EPA - Vehicle emissions comparisons
  • MLPerf - Industry-standard AI inference benchmarks
